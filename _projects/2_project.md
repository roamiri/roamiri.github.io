## Digital Twin Network

A **digital twin network** is a real-time virtual replica of a physical wireless network — synchronized with live hardware, capable of running AI experiments, and enabling what-if analysis without touching the real system.

At Qualcomm Wireless R&D, I contributed to the construction, validation, and deployment of a digital twin of an indoor factory environment, showcased live at **MWC 2024** in Barcelona. The system runs alongside a physical 5G over-the-air testbed in San Diego.

### Key Capabilities

- **High-fidelity ray-tracing propagation model** synchronized with the physical environment
- **Real-time channel model calibration** using uplink channel state information from live UEs
- **AI-driven network optimization** — scheduling, beamforming, and interference management policies trained in the digital twin and transferred to the live network
- **Multi-layer validation pipeline** comparing simulated and measured KPIs at scale
