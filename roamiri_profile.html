Research Interests

⚡

Model Quantization & Efficient AI

Mixed-precision quantization, model compression, and neural architecture search for energy-efficient inference on mobile and edge devices, including self-driving AI platforms.

📡

AI-Native Wireless Positioning

Neural networks for indoor localization, RF-SLAM, and environment mapping using 5G channel state information — enabling sub-meter accuracy without GPS.

🌐

6G Network Intelligence

Digital twin construction and validation for next-generation wireless networks; AI-driven scheduling, interference management, and heterogeneous network optimization.

🤖

Deep RL for Communications

Distributed multi-agent reinforcement learning for power allocation and spectrum management in dense interference-limited networks.
